The Importance of Volunteering in the Community

A short look at what volunteering is, what it gives back — to the person who gives and to the place they live in — and how to start locally.

01 · Introduction

Giving time, not money

If everyone in this room gave just two hours a month — what would our campus look like in a year?

  • A
    What it is

    Volunteering means giving your time and effort to help others, with no payment expected in return.

  • B
    Why it matters to you

    It builds character, practical skills and real connections — things a classroom alone cannot give.

  • C
    Why it matters to society

    It fills the gaps that governments and businesses cannot reach, and it keeps social bonds strong.

02 · Benefits — Personal

What you take home

  • 01
    New skills

    Communication, leadership, teamwork — learned by doing, under real pressure.

  • 02
    A wider network

    You meet people outside your usual circle: other students, staff, locals, professionals.

  • 03
    Better mental health

    Helping others creates a sense of purpose and lowers stress — you feel useful.

  • 04
    A stronger CV

    Employers read volunteering as proof of initiative, not just as a line to fill space.

03 · Benefits — Community

What the community gains

  • 01
    Direct help for people in need

    Food, clothes, tutoring, care — support that reaches someone this week, not next year.

  • 02
    Pressure on real social problems

    Poverty, the environment, unequal access to education — volunteers keep these visible.

  • 03
    A community that holds together

    People who work side by side start to trust each other. That trust is the safety net.

04 · Popular volunteer activities

Three ways people actually start

Food drives

Collect & share food

Gather donations and hand them out to families, shelters or workers who need them.

Clean-ups

Clean a shared space

Beaches, parks, canals, campus. Low barrier, visible result, easy to do as a group.

Tutoring

Teach what you know

Free lessons for children and students who cannot afford extra classes.

06 · Local opportunities

Where to sign up, near you

You do not have to look far. Most of these run every year and are always short of hands.

On campus

Youth Union & Student Association

The usual entry point: they organise most student volunteer work and announce it first.

Summer

Green Summer (Mùa hè xanh)

The long-running student campaign — building, teaching and community work in rural areas.

Before Tet

Volunteer Spring (Xuân tình nguyện)

Gifts, meals and events for families and workers who stay in the city over the holiday.
